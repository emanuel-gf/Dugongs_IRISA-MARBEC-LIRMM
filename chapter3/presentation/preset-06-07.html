Methodology¶

Binary classification of tiles with respect different input dimensions.

Initial Conditions:

  • NC dataset has a widthxheight of 2704x1520. So a tile of 2048 is not possible. | EXCLUDED
  • WPmix is a reduced dataset with mixed Manual and FLPLAN flights from the subregions: UM, GAM, MANTASANDY, FRIWEN. The size of the images are: (5472,3648) (4096,2160) (3840,2160), (4000,3000).
  • UMflplan. Only transects flights. TEST SET. Sizes: (5472,3648) (5280,3956), (4000,3000).

-- WPmix and UMflplan are tiled into 1024x1024 with 224 overlapping AND 2048x2048 with 224 overlapping. From each tile, a forward pass of Dinov3 model is done to retrieve the embeddings.

All classification are based on these embeddings.

TRAIN: Wpmix | TEST: UM_flplan

--

Tiling Process: Giving a stride of size (1024 OR 2048) and 224 overlap, the images are tiled into smaller images. The condition of either presence or absence of dugongs is given by solely if it contains a bbox of dugong or not within the image tile. However, boundaries tile-regions can be tricky, therefore, during the overlap, if the bbox representing the dugong is reduced more than a threshold area ratio (0.45) that bbox is therefore desconsidered. Allowing only bbox with at least 55% percent of area remaining to be considered a positive label.

This avoids the tiles of being considered positives and only containing a very small fraction of the dugong. However, on the other hand, it may flag as negative some tiles that may contain a distinct visual feature, for instance, the tail of a dugong.
